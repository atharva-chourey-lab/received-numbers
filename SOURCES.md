## 4. Gaps flagged in the paper

1. **Textbook-misattribution survey missing.** No source file surveys modern textbooks crediting Xuanzang directly with the 10,000. Paper §3.2 and §6.2(8) state the reframing and flag the gap; no textbook examples are cited.
2. **Data/code availability beyond the files** (repository, DOI, licence) is not stated in any source file; paper claims none.
3. **Bodhidharma (C1-PRE addendum, Case 16)** was run as a dedicated build outside both tallies; its files were not among this paper's sources, so it is mentioned only to explain its exclusion from the denominator.
4. Lag constant is stated differently in C1 (≈160 yrs) and C2 (≈160–186 yrs, n=5); paper reports both rather than harmonising.
